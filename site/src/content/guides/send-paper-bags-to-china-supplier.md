If your products are made in China, custom paper bags can be delivered to your product supplier, packer, warehouse or consolidation point before export. This lets the final product, box, tissue paper, card and bag meet in one local workflow.

This is useful for retail-ready products, gift sets, event orders and ecommerce brands that want to reduce repacking work after goods arrive overseas.

## When this route makes sense

Send paper bags to a China supplier when the supplier already handles final product packing or when several packaging parts need to be grouped before shipment.

For example, your product factory may receive [custom paper bags](/products/custom-paper-bags/), product boxes, tissue paper and printed cards. They can pack the finished unit before handing cartons to your freight route.

## Checklist before delivery

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier address and contact | Prevents cartons waiting at the wrong receiving point. |
| Bag carton count | Helps the supplier plan storage space. |
| Packing instruction | Explains how each product should sit inside the bag. |
| SKU or order reference | Helps match bags with the correct product batch. |
| Handoff date | Keeps bags from arriving too early or too late. |

## What your supplier should confirm

Your product supplier should confirm they can receive the bags, store them safely, pack products into them and follow the packing instruction. They should also confirm whether they need labels, carton marks or SKU separation.

If several suppliers are involved, use one receiving warehouse or consolidation point when possible. It reduces confusion and keeps packing records cleaner.

## Watch points

- Paper bags usually ship flat, but cartons still take storage space.
- Luxury bags can be easier to dent, so carton handling matters.
- Bags should arrive before product packing starts, not after.
- If products are heavy, handle strength should be checked before the bags are delivered.
- If Amazon or retail labels are needed, confirm who applies them.

## FAQ

### Can paper bags be delivered together with custom boxes?

Yes. Paper bags, boxes, inserts and printed cards can be delivered together or separately depending on the supplier and packing plan.

### Who should pack the product into the paper bag?

Usually the product supplier, packer or consolidation warehouse handles final packing. The right route depends on who has the product and who controls final inspection before export.
