Luxury perfume rigid boxes are useful when the packaging must feel gift-ready at first opening. The structure, insert and finish should be sampled before bulk production.

![Premium rigid boxes for luxury perfume packaging](/images/products/custom-rigid-boxes.jpg)

## Rigid box checks

| Detail | Watch point |
| --- | --- |
| Box style | Drawer, lid-and-base or magnetic structure |
| Insert | Bottle support and accessory layout |
| Finish | Foil, embossing, debossing or spot UV |
| Material | Wrapped paper, board thickness and surface feel |
| Freight volume | Rigid boxes ship larger than flat cartons |

Use rigid boxes where the retail price and launch positioning justify the extra size and cost.
