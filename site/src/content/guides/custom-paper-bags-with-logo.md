Custom paper bags with logo are easiest to quote when you send the bag size, product weight, handle type, paper preference, print method and where the bags need to go after production. If the bags will be used with product boxes, send the box size too.

For ecommerce and retail brands sourcing from China, paper bags can be planned together with [custom paper boxes](/products/) and delivered to your product factory, packer or warehouse before final handoff.

## What to prepare first

Start with the packed product, not only the bag design. A bag that looks good on a screen can fail if the box inside is too wide, too heavy or packed in the wrong direction.

| Quote detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Bag width, height and depth | Controls product fit and carton packing. |
| Product or box size | Prevents bags that are too tight or too loose. |
| Packed weight | Affects paper thickness, handle and reinforcement. |
| Handle type | Changes appearance, cost and strength. |
| Artwork | Decides whether simple logo print, full color or foil is practical. |
| Delivery point | Bags may go to your supplier, packer, warehouse or freight route in China. |

## Common logo options

Simple logo printing works for most paper bag projects. It is usually cleaner and easier to control than heavy full-coverage artwork.

For a more premium result, use foil stamping, embossing, debossing or a focused border line. Luxury bags often look better when the logo area is controlled instead of filling every panel.

## Paper and handle choices

White paper bags are better when clean color printing matters. [Kraft paper bags](/products/custom-kraft-paper-bags/) are better when the brand wants a natural paper look. [Luxury paper gift bags](/products/luxury-paper-gift-bags/) may use thicker paper, ribbon handles and reinforced handle holes.

Handle options usually include twisted paper handles, rope handles, cotton handles and ribbon handles. The right choice depends on packed weight, brand look and how the bag will be carried.

## When bags should be delivered in China

If your products are made in China, it may be practical to send finished bags to your product supplier. The supplier can pack the product box, tissue, card or gift set into the paper bag before export.

This route is useful when you want less repacking work after the goods arrive overseas. See [send packaging to your China supplier](/send-packaging-to-china-supplier/) if the bags need to move to another factory or warehouse.

## Before asking for a quote

- Send bag size or the product box size.
- Confirm product weight inside each bag.
- Choose kraft, white paper, black paper or specialty paper.
- Choose twisted paper, rope, cotton or ribbon handles.
- Send artwork as AI, PDF or a clear logo file.
- Explain whether bags ship empty, with boxes, or to your China supplier.

## FAQ

### What is the best paper for logo printed paper bags?

White card paper is flexible for clean color printing. Brown kraft paper works when a natural look is more important than exact color matching.

### Can paper bags be packed with my product boxes?

Yes. Paper bags can be delivered with boxes or sent to a China supplier, packer or warehouse depending on the project route.
