Glass jar candle packaging needs to control movement. The candle may look small, but the jar weight, lid, glass edge and shipping route can make the packaging harder than a normal paper box.

For a useful quote, send the jar diameter, jar height, jar weight, lid shape, quantity and shipping route.

![Black candle box with fitted insert for glass jar protection](/images/guides/supplier-black-candle-box-insert.jpg)

## Protection options

| Option | Best for | Watch point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Snug folding carton | Single retail candle | Needs accurate jar dimensions |
| Paperboard insert | Gift box or light jar | Test movement before bulk order |
| Divider | Multiple candles in one box | Keep jars separated during handling |
| Rigid box | Premium candle gift | Stronger presentation but higher freight volume |
| Outer shipping carton | Ecommerce or export route | Needed when retail box is not enough |

## Plan around real handling

A candle box that looks good on a table may not survive parcel handling alone. If the candle will ship directly to customers, compare the retail box and the outer shipping carton together.

For gift sets, confirm the candle order, insert cutouts and card position before artwork approval.
