Skincare gift set packaging should start with product count, product weight and the way the set will be opened. A serum bottle, cream jar, tube and sample vial may fit in one box, but they should not be planned as if they are one simple carton.

For a useful quote, prepare the product sizes, product weights, set layout, box style, artwork stage, order quantity, destination and whether the products need to be packed in China.

![Skincare gift set packaging boxes with printed paper inserts](/images/industries/custom-skincare-boxes.jpg)

## Common structures for skincare gift sets

| Set type | Common packaging choice | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium skincare set | Lid-and-base rigid box or drawer box | Strong presentation and better opening feel |
| Trial or sample set | Folding carton, sleeve or mailer kit | Lower cost for smaller launch quantities |
| Ecommerce beauty bundle | Printed mailer box with insert | Good for direct shipping and brand presentation |
| Retail shelf set | Folding carton with paper insert | Easier to stack and display |

## Plan the insert before artwork

The insert controls how the products sit in the box. If the insert changes after artwork approval, the box size may also change.

Paperboard inserts and wrapped paper inserts are common for skincare jars, bottles and small tubes. Heavier glass containers may need extra testing, especially when the gift set will move through ecommerce or freight forwarding routes.

See: [paper inserts vs plastic trays](/guides/paper-inserts-vs-plastic-trays/) and [custom paper inserts](/products/custom-paper-inserts/).

## Decide whether packing in China helps

If your skincare products are made in China, it may be practical to make the packaging, receive or coordinate the products, pack suitable sets, and prepare carton notes before the goods move to the next route.

This can reduce repeated handling and make label, carton and freight details easier to control.

See: [product packing service in China](/product-packing-service-china/) and [cosmetic gift set packaging from China](/cosmetic-gift-set-packaging-china/).

## Quote checklist

- Product photos, size and weight for each skincare item
- Quantity per set and total order quantity
- Box style, insert style and opening direction
- Artwork, logo files and finish ideas
- Barcode, label, carton mark or FBA note requirements
- Destination country and preferred freight route
