Food-safe paper packaging starts with one question: will the food touch the paper directly? If yes, material, liner and document requirements should be discussed before quoting.

![Paper food packaging boxes with retail window display](/images/products/custom-gable-boxes.jpg)

## Details to clarify

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Direct food contact | Material and liner requirements may change |
| Grease or moisture | Paper treatment or inner wrap may be needed |
| Window area | Confirm whether food can touch the window material |
| Destination market | Food packaging requirements can differ |
| Product shelf life | Packaging may need extra barrier discussion |

ZiiBoxes can discuss the paper box, structure, liner and carton details. Formal food-contact compliance should be confirmed against your destination requirement.
