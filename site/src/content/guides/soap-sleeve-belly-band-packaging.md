Soap sleeves and belly bands use less paper than full boxes and can make handmade bars feel simple and natural. They need accurate product size and clear wrapping steps.

![Kraft product box with printed belly band](/images/guides/supplier-belly-band-packaging.jpg)

## Sleeve checks

| Detail | Watch point |
| --- | --- |
| Bar circumference | Controls band length |
| Paper thickness | Affects folding and fit |
| Label copy | Scent, weight and ingredient space |
| Packing speed | Bands should be easy to apply consistently |
| Product variation | Handmade bars may vary in size |

Sleeves work best when the soap itself is strong enough and does not need a full protective box.
