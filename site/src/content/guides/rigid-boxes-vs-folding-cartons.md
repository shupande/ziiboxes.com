Rigid boxes and folding cartons both work for retail packaging, but they sit at different levels of cost, structure and presentation. Folding cartons are lightweight and efficient. Rigid boxes feel more premium but take more space and usually cost more.

The right choice depends on product price, sales channel, product weight and how much the unboxing experience matters.

## Main differences

| Factor | Folding carton | Rigid box |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structure | Folded paperboard | Thick greyboard wrapped with paper |
| Storage | Ships flat before filling | Usually does not fold flat |
| Cost | Usually lower | Usually higher |
| Best for | Retail products, cosmetics, soap, food sleeves | Gift sets, perfume, jewelry, premium kits |
| Inserts | Paperboard insert or simple divider | Paper, foam, pulp-style or custom insert |

## Choose folding cartons when unit cost matters

[Custom folding cartons](/products/custom-folding-cartons/) are practical for lipstick, soap, supplements, small food products and other retail items. They are easier to store, efficient for repeat orders and useful when the product already has primary packaging.

Folding cartons are a good fit when:

- The product is lightweight
- Shelf display matters
- You need a printed outer box
- You want a lower landed cost
- The product will be packed into a separate shipping carton

## Choose rigid boxes when presentation matters more

[Custom rigid boxes](/products/custom-rigid-boxes/) are better for premium launches, gift sets, jewelry, fragrance and higher-value products. The structure feels more substantial and can hold inserts more elegantly.

Rigid boxes are a good fit when:

- The packaging is part of the product value
- The item is a gift set or premium kit
- You need a stronger presentation box
- The brand wants foil, embossing or specialty paper

## Watch the freight cost

Rigid boxes often take more space because they do not fold flat. That can change shipping cost and warehouse planning. If you are comparing quotes, ask for packed carton dimensions and total gross weight, not only unit price.
