Cake pop and macaron packaging needs spacing. Small delicate items can move, crack or lose presentation if the insert or divider is not planned early.

![Bakery gift boxes for delicate food packaging](/images/industries/custom-food-packaging-boxes.jpg)

## Delicate food checks

| Product | Packaging check |
| --- | --- |
| Macarons | Divider spacing and window alignment |
| Cake pops | Stick position and insert hole size |
| Small cookies | Tray or paper divider |
| Gift set | Card, sleeve and label position |
| Shipping order | Outer carton and cushion plan |

Send real product dimensions rather than only a photo. Small bakery items often need accurate spacing.
