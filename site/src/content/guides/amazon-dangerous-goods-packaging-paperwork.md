If a product may be treated as dangerous goods, sensitive cargo or a regulated item, raise that point before packaging production and packing. The packaging supplier should not guess the classification after cartons are already packed.

Your Amazon account, freight forwarder, customs broker or testing provider should confirm the final requirement. ZiiBoxes can help keep the packaging, packing notes, carton details and handoff paperwork aligned with the route you choose.

## Products to flag early

| Product type | Why to flag it before packing |
| --- | --- |
| Liquids and gels | Leakage, sealing and document checks may be needed |
| Fragrance or scented products | Freight and marketplace routes may ask for extra information |
| Batteries or electronics | Labels, documents and freight route limits may apply |
| Powders or granular products | Customs and carrier checks may need clearer paperwork |
| Magnetic products | Some air freight routes may require special checks |
| Fragile glass items | Packing method and carton protection should be planned early |

## What to send before quoting

- Product photos and product description
- Ingredient, battery, liquid, powder or material notes if relevant
- Destination country and sales channel
- Freight route or logistics contact if already chosen
- Any document request from Amazon, customs, carrier or warehouse
