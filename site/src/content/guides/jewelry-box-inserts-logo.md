Custom jewelry boxes with logo need to protect small products and create a premium first impression. The most important early decision is not only the logo finish. It is the structure and insert that hold the jewelry in place.

Send a product photo, product size, quantity and logo file before asking for a quote. For necklaces, bracelets, earrings and rings, the insert can change the whole box layout.

## Common jewelry box choices

| Jewelry product | Packaging to consider |
| --- | --- |
| Rings and earrings | Small rigid box, drawer box or insert box |
| Necklaces | Rigid box with card insert or velvet-style insert |
| Bracelets | Drawer box, rigid box or sleeve set |
| Gift sets | Rigid gift box with divided insert |
| Online orders | Small box inside mailer or outer shipping carton |

## Logo finish options

Foil stamping is common for jewelry packaging because it gives a clear premium mark without needing full-color print. Embossing, debossing, matte lamination and specialty paper can also work.

Keep the logo effect simple if this is the first order. A small, well-placed logo is easier to control than a large finish area.

## Inserts matter more than they look

The insert controls how the product sits when the box opens. A poor insert can make a premium box feel unfinished. If you sell several jewelry sizes, ask whether one box structure can use different inserts across SKUs.
