Amazon FBA packaging prep in China is mostly about avoiding missing details before goods move. The packaging supplier can help with boxes, inserts, labels and carton data, but your Amazon account or logistics provider should confirm the final FBA requirements.

Use this checklist before printing boxes or packing finished products.

![Custom ecommerce packaging prepared for Amazon seller shipments](/images/guides/supplier-delivery-packaging-boxes.jpg)

## Prep checklist

| Prep area | Details to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Unit packaging | Product box style, product protection, insert and closure method |
| Barcode or FNSKU | Label size, position, files and SKU instructions from your route |
| Set or bundle | How many products go into one sellable unit |
| Master carton | Units per carton, carton size, carton weight and carton count |
| Packing list | SKU, quantity, carton count and handoff notes |
| Delivery route | China supplier, warehouse, prep provider, freight forwarder or FBA route |

## What ZiiBoxes can help with

ZiiBoxes can make the printed boxes, reserve barcode space, produce inserts, discuss product packing, prepare carton details and hand off packed goods to your confirmed China route.

If you do not already have a logistics route, suitable freight forwarder or warehouse contacts can be discussed. Final shipping, customs and Amazon account work stay with the logistics provider or your team.

## What your team should confirm

Confirm the final SKU data, barcode file, FNSKU file, shipment label, warehouse address, appointment requirement and any special product category rule through your Amazon account or logistics partner.
