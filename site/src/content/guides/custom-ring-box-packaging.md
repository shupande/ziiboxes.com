Custom ring box packaging should feel compact, secure and gift-ready. The insert, lid opening and logo scale matter more than adding many decorative effects.

![Ring jewelry box with insert and premium presentation](/images/jewelry-boxes.jpg)

## Ring box planning

| Detail | Watch point |
| --- | --- |
| Box size | Ring height and insert compression |
| Insert slot | Ring angle and stability |
| Logo size | Keep foil or print readable |
| Surface finish | Matte, soft-touch, foil or embossing |
| Carton packing | Avoid crushing small boxes in bulk cartons |

If the ring box will be packed with a card, pouch or certificate, send those sizes before sampling.
