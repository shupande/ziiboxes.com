Scented candle packaging should be planned with the shipping route in mind. A freight forwarder, warehouse or customs broker may ask for product details before the goods move, especially when the candle is scented, glass, heavy or part of a mixed gift set.

The final shipping rule should come from your logistics route. ZiiBoxes can prepare packaging-side packing notes, carton details and handoff information.

![Closed scented candle gift box prepared before freight handoff](/images/guides/supplier-closed-scented-candle-box.jpg)

## Details to flag early

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Scented product | Route may ask for clearer product notes |
| Glass jar | Protection and carton handling need attention |
| Candle weight | Affects insert strength and carton count |
| Mixed gift set | SKU and packing list details should stay clear |
| Destination country | Warehouse, customs and carrier requirements can differ |
| Freight route | Sea, air, courier or warehouse route may request different details |

## Before packing

Send product photos, scent or material notes if requested, jar size, jar weight, carton limits and any document request from your logistics provider.

If products are packed in China, carton size, carton weight, carton count, SKU notes and packing list information can be prepared after packing.
