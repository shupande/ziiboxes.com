Packaging cost is not only the unit price of the box. For overseas buyers, box size and packed carton volume can change the landed cost. A box that looks cheap per unit may become expensive after freight is included.

Ask for packed carton dimensions and gross weight when comparing quotes, especially for rigid boxes, gift boxes and assembled packaging.

## Why size matters

Shipping charges often consider both actual weight and volume. Paper packaging can be bulky even when it is not heavy. Rigid boxes and assembled gift boxes usually take more space than flat folding cartons.

| Packaging type | Freight note |
| --- | --- |
| Folding cartons | Usually ship flat and pack efficiently |
| Mailer boxes | Ship flat, but larger panels increase carton size |
| Rigid boxes | Usually take more space because they do not fold flat |
| Gift sets | Inserts, sleeves and accessories can add volume |
| Shipping boxes | Board strength and size affect carton packing |

## Compare landed cost

When comparing suppliers, ask whether the quote includes:

- Unit price
- Sample cost
- Packed carton size
- Gross weight
- Freight estimate
- Destination country

Without these details, the lowest unit price may not be the lowest delivered cost.

## Right-size the box

Right-sizing helps reduce material waste, storage space and freight volume. Send product size and product weight before asking for a quote. If one box needs to fit several products, explain the size range so the structure can be planned.
