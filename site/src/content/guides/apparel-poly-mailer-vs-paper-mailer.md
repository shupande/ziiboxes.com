Paper mailer boxes and poly mailers solve different problems. Paper packaging gives a stronger branded feel, while poly mailers may be lighter for some garment shipping routes.

![Custom mailer boxes for apparel ecommerce packaging](/images/products/custom-mailer-boxes.jpg)

## Comparison

| Option | Good for | Watch point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper mailer box | DTC unboxing and gift orders | Higher volume than soft mailers |
| Paper sleeve | Retail-style folded garments | Needs outer shipping protection |
| Belly band | Simple branding around folded product | Less protection |
| Poly mailer route | Lightweight shipping | Brand and sustainability goals may differ |
| Mailer plus card | Better customer experience | Packing steps need clarity |

Choose based on product value, shipping route and brand expectations, not only unit packaging price.
