Socks and underwear packaging often needs small-format boxes, sleeves or cards. The size range and barcode placement should be confirmed before artwork approval.

![Custom apparel packaging boxes for small clothing products](/images/industries/custom-apparel-packaging-boxes.jpg)

## Packaging checks

| Product | Packaging option |
| --- | --- |
| Socks | Sleeve, folding carton or hanging card |
| Underwear | Folding carton, drawer box or sleeve |
| Multi-pack | Divider, band or larger folding carton |
| Retail display | Hook hole, barcode and size mark |
| Ecommerce order | Inner box plus mailer or shipping carton |

Keep size labels consistent if the same artwork is used across several SKUs.
