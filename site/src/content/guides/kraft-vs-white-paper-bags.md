Choose kraft paper bags when your brand wants a natural, simple paper look. Choose white paper bags when clean color printing, premium checkout presentation or accurate brand color matters more.

Both options can work for retail, ecommerce, gift sets and event handoff. The best choice depends on artwork, product weight, handle style and whether the bag should match a box, sleeve or tissue paper.

## Quick comparison

| Option | Best for | Watch point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brown kraft paper bags | Natural brands, soap, candles, food gifts, market-style retail | Full-color artwork looks different on brown paper. |
| White paper bags | Boutiques, beauty, apparel, clean logo printing | Dirt and handling marks show more easily. |
| Black paper bags | Luxury retail, wine, premium gift sets | Foil or light artwork is usually needed for contrast. |
| Printed kraft bags | Natural paper feel with simple color blocks | Keep artwork bold and not too detailed. |

## When kraft paper bags make sense

[Kraft paper bags](/products/custom-kraft-paper-bags/) fit brands that want a less glossy, more grounded retail feel. They work well for handmade soap, candles, natural food gifts, apparel pickup and simple market packaging.

Kraft paper is not a shortcut to every environmental claim. If your buyer needs a specific recycled content, coating, ink or compliance document, confirm that requirement before sampling.

## When white paper bags make sense

[White paper shopping bags](/products/custom-shopping-bags/) are better when the logo color, full-color artwork or premium store handoff needs to look clean. White paper also gives more contrast for soft colors, gold foil or subtle pattern work.

White bags often work well with cosmetics, skincare, boutiques, gift shops and event merchandise.

## How artwork changes the decision

If your logo is black, dark green, navy or one simple color, kraft paper can look clean. If your logo uses soft colors, gradients, detailed photos or exact brand colors, white paper is usually safer.

Ask for a sample if color accuracy matters. A brown paper base changes how ink appears, even when the digital file looks correct.

## China-side delivery note

If your product supplier in China handles final packing, bags can be delivered flat-packed to that supplier. This is useful when boxes, tissue paper, cards and bags need to meet at one packing point.

For this route, confirm who receives the bags, how many bags go into each carton and whether your supplier has space to store them before packing.

## FAQ

### Are kraft paper bags cheaper than white paper bags?

Sometimes, but not always. Price depends on paper weight, size, handle, print coverage, finish and quantity.

### Which paper bag is better for luxury brands?

White, black or specialty paper bags usually fit luxury better than plain kraft, especially when paired with foil, ribbon handles or a rigid gift box.
