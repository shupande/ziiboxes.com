A cosmetic gift set packaging quote is faster and more accurate when the box supplier can see the full set, not only the outside box size. The product mix, insert layout, artwork, label details and packing route all affect the price.

If your products are made in China, also say whether the finished boxes should go to your factory, warehouse, freight forwarder or Amazon route.

![Cosmetic packaging boxes and product cartons prepared for quote details](/images/guides/pexels-cosmetic-boxes.jpg)

## Product details to send

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Product photo | Helps confirm shape, finish and packing risk |
| Product size | Controls the box and insert dimensions |
| Product weight | Affects insert strength and export carton planning |
| Items per set | Decides the insert layout and packing time |
| Primary package material | Glass, plastic and metal containers need different checks |

## Packaging details to send

Tell the supplier whether you want a rigid box, drawer box, folding carton, sleeve, window box or mailer kit. If you are not sure, send a target retail price and sales channel.

For cosmetic gift sets, the insert should be discussed before final artwork. A small change in bottle spacing can change the box size, carton quantity and freight volume.

## Artwork and label details

Send logo files, dieline files if available, color requirements and finish ideas. Also mention barcode labels, carton marks, FBA labels or any platform-specific packing notes.

If the packaging will be sent to your China product supplier, provide the factory city and receiving contact before shipment.

## Packing and freight details

| Route | Details to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Send empty boxes to a China supplier | Factory address, contact, delivery time and carton quantity |
| Pack finished gift sets in China | Product arrival plan, packing steps, label needs and quality check points |
| Hand off to freight forwarder | Carton size, carton weight, carton count and shipping marks |
| Amazon or ecommerce warehouse route | Label rules, carton limits and paperwork needs from the logistics provider |

## Common mistakes

- Asking for a price before confirming product size and weight
- Approving artwork before checking insert layout
- Comparing suppliers without the same material and finish details
- Forgetting carton size, carton weight and label requirements
