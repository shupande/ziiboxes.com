Printing finishes change how a custom paper box looks and feels. They also change production cost, sampling time and sometimes minimum order quantity. The safest choice is to pick the finish that supports the product position, not the finish that adds the most effects.

## Matte and gloss lamination

Matte lamination gives a softer, less reflective surface. Gloss lamination makes color look brighter and more reflective. Both can help protect printed paper from light handling.

Matte is common for cosmetic boxes, gift boxes and brands that want a calmer look. Gloss is useful when bright color and shine are important. For kraft paper, the natural paper texture may be more important than heavy surface treatment.

## Foil stamping

Foil stamping is often used for logos, lines or premium details. Gold and silver are common, but other foil colors can be used when available.

Foil works best when it is focused. A small foil logo can look more premium than a large foil area that is hard to control.

## Spot UV and embossing

Spot UV adds shine to selected areas. Embossing raises part of the paper surface. These finishes are common on cosmetic boxes, gift boxes and premium rigid boxes.

| Finish | Best use | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matte lamination | Soft retail look, cosmetic boxes, gift boxes | Fingerprints can show on some dark colors |
| Gloss lamination | Bright color, shiny display boxes | Can feel less subtle on premium products |
| Foil stamping | Logos, lines, premium accents | Large foil areas may increase setup complexity |
| Spot UV | Highlighting a logo or pattern | Works best when artwork has enough contrast |
| Embossing | Raised brand marks or texture | Needs enough paper thickness and clear artwork |

## How to choose finishes for a first order

If this is your first custom packaging order, keep the finish plan simple. Choose one main finish and one optional accent. This makes the sample easier to evaluate and keeps the quote easier to compare.

Good starting combinations:

- Folding carton: matte lamination plus small foil logo
- Rigid box: specialty paper plus embossing or foil
- Mailer box: outside CMYK print plus simple inside message
- Soap box: kraft paper plus window or one-color print

## FAQ

### Do finishes affect production time?

They can. Foil, embossing, spot UV and special lamination add production steps, so they should be confirmed before sampling.

### Should every premium box use foil?

No. Premium packaging can also come from good structure, paper texture, color control and a clean dieline.
