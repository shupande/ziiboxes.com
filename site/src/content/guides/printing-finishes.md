Printing finishes change how a custom paper box looks and feels. They also change production cost, sampling time and sometimes minimum order quantity.

## Matte and gloss lamination

Matte lamination gives a softer, less reflective surface. Gloss lamination makes color look brighter and more reflective. Both can help protect printed paper from light handling.

## Foil stamping

Foil stamping is often used for logos, lines or premium details. Gold and silver are common, but other foil colors can be used when available.

## Spot UV and embossing

Spot UV adds shine to selected areas. Embossing raises part of the paper surface. These finishes are common on cosmetic boxes, gift boxes and premium rigid boxes.
