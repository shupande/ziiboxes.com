You just opened a quote for custom paper boxes, and your supplier asks whether you want an FSC label printed on the package. You have seen the small tree-check logo on coffee cups and retail bags, but you are not sure what the label actually certifies—or whether your brand needs it.

The practical answer: FSC labels on [FSC labels packaging](https://us.fsc.org/what-is-fsc/what-do-the-fsc-labels-mean) show that the paper comes from responsibly managed forests or verified recycled sources. You should request the label when your brand makes sustainability claims, sells to retailers with environmental procurement policies, or wants to verify that your paper sourcing is not linked to illegal logging.

If you are ordering custom [paper bags with your logo](/guides/custom-paper-bags-with-logo/) or folding cartons, you do not always need the label. However, if you plan to print eco-friendly messaging on the packaging, the label provides proof. This guide explains the three label types, when to use them, and how to request them from your packaging supplier.

## How FSC Labels Work for Paper Packaging

The Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) is an international non-profit that sets standards for responsible forest management. When you see an FSC label on paper packaging, it means an independent auditor has traced the material from the forest to the finished product. The [US EPA recognizes sustainable packaging](https://www.epa.gov/smm/sustainable-packaging) as a way to reduce environmental impacts while recovering economic value from discarded materials. FSC certification is one way to verify that your packaging supports those goals.

For paper packaging specifically, the label confirms the fiber source. This includes the kraft paper used for [custom kraft paper bags](/products/custom-kraft-paper-bags/) and the paperboard used for folding cartons. The label tells your customers that a third party has verified the origin of the raw material.

It is important to understand that an FSC label is different from a generic recycling symbol. A generic mobius loop might tell a customer the package is recyclable in theory. An FSC label tells them where the fiber came from before it reached the printer.

## The Three FSC Label Types Explained

FSC offers three distinct labels. Each one represents a different material composition. If you want to use a label on your custom paper packaging, you need to know which one matches your material choice.

### FSC 100%

The FSC 100% label means the packaging is made entirely from virgin fiber sourced from FSC-certified forests. These forests have been managed according to strict environmental and social standards. If your brand wants to support responsible forestry and you prefer virgin fiber for brightness or strength, this is the label to request.

### FSC Mix

The FSC Mix label means the packaging contains a combination of materials. According to the [Forest Stewardship Council](https://us.fsc.org/what-is-fsc/what-do-the-fsc-labels-mean), this mix can include FSC-certified virgin fiber, recycled fiber, and controlled virgin fiber. Controlled fiber has been assessed to ensure a low risk of coming from illegal logging, human rights violations, or the conversion of natural forests into plantations. This label is common when a supplier blends fibers to meet cost or performance requirements.

### FSC Recycled

The FSC Recycled label means the packaging is made from 100% recycled fiber. A mobius loop in the corner shows the recycled percentage. This label differs from general recycled claims because it requires verification. All the wood or paper in the product has been confirmed as genuinely recycled. If your brand wants to emphasize waste reduction and does not need virgin fiber, request this label.

## When to Request FSC Labels Packaging

You do not need an FSC label on every custom box or bag. However, there are specific situations where it makes sense to ask for it.

### When You Make Sustainability Claims

If you print eco-friendly messages on your [custom food packaging boxes](/industries/custom-food-packaging-boxes/) or mailer boxes, you need proof. An FSC label backs up your claim with third-party verification. This protects your brand from accusations of greenwashing, which is when a company falsely markets its products as environmentally friendly.

### When Selling to Retail Buyers

Many large retailers have sustainable procurement policies. If you pitch your products to chain stores, they may ask for FSC-certified packaging as part of their vendor requirements. Having the label ready on your packaging speeds up the onboarding process.

### When You Need to Match Brand Values

If your brand identity is built around environmental responsibility, the FSC label is a visual cue. Customers who care about forestry will recognize it. If your target audience does not prioritize sustainability, the label may not drive additional sales. In that case, you might prioritize other packaging features like structural durability or finish effects.

## How to Request FSC Labels from Your Supplier

To get an FSC label on your custom paper packaging, you need to communicate clearly with your supplier. A supplier like ZiiBoxes can discuss product packing, carton details, and artwork, but the FSC label request needs to be specific.

### Ask About Chain of Custody Certification

The supplier must have a Chain of Custody (CoC) certificate. This certificate proves they can trace the FSC-certified material through their production process. Ask your supplier for their CoC certificate number before you commit to an order. Without it, they cannot legally print the label on your boxes.

### Specify the Label in Your Artwork

You need to decide which of the three labels you want. You also need to tell the supplier where to place it. Common placements are the bottom of the box or the back panel of a bag. The FSC has strict rules about how the label appears, including size and color. Your supplier should have the correct artwork files.

### Confirm the Paper Source

Your supplier must source the paper from an FSC-certified mill. If you want FSC Recycled, they need to purchase certified recycled board. Ask for the mill name and certification details to confirm the material matches your label choice.

## Purchasing and Specification Checklist

Use this checklist before you [request a quote](/request-a-quote/) for custom paper packaging:

- **Label Choice**: Decide between FSC 100%, FSC Mix, or FSC Recycled.
- **Supplier Certificate**: Ask your supplier for their Chain of Custody certificate number.
- **Material Verification**: Request the mill name and fiber source details.
- **Artwork Placement**: Choose where the label will appear on the box or bag.
- **Label Files**: Confirm the supplier has the official FSC artwork files.
- **Retail Requirements**: Check if your retail partners require a specific label type.
- **Eco Claims**: Ensure any printed sustainability claims match the label you choose.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

When ordering FSC labels packaging, buyers often make a few avoidable mistakes.

### Assuming All Brown Paper is FSC Certified

Brown kraft paper looks natural, but that does not mean it is FSC-certified. The color comes from the pulping process, not from the forest source. If you want the label, you must request certified material. Unverified kraft paper could come from any forest, including illegally harvested sources.

### Using the Label Without a Supplier Certificate

You cannot download an FSC logo from the internet and place it on your box. The FSC controls the trademark. If your supplier does not have a Chain of Custody certificate, you cannot use the label. Using it without authorization can lead to legal action and damage your brand reputation.

### Mismatching Labels and Materials

If you request an FSC Recycled label but your supplier uses a mix of virgin and recycled fiber, the label is invalid. The label must accurately reflect the material in the box. Always confirm the paper composition with your supplier before approving the artwork.

## Making the Decision for Your Brand

Choosing the right FSC label for your custom paper packaging comes down to your brand values, your customer base, and your supplier capabilities. If your customers care about forestry, choose FSC 100%. If you want to balance cost and environmental responsibility, choose FSC Mix. If your brand focuses on waste reduction, choose FSC Recycled.

The FSC label is a tool for building trust. It shows customers that you have invested in verified sustainable materials. For small and medium-sized brands, it can be a differentiator in a crowded market. Just remember that the label requires a certified supplier and accurate material sourcing. If you are ready to move forward, prepare your specification checklist and ask your supplier for their Chain of Custody details.

## FAQ

### What does FSC mean on packaging?
FSC stands for Forest Stewardship Council. On packaging, it means the paper or wood fiber comes from forests managed according to strict environmental and social standards verified by an independent third party.

### What is the difference between FSC Mix and FSC Recycled?
FSC Mix contains a combination of FSC-certified virgin fiber, controlled fiber, and sometimes recycled fiber. FSC Recycled means 100% of the fiber has been verified as genuinely recycled material.

### Do I need an FSC label for my custom paper bags?
You do not need it. However, if you make eco-friendly claims or sell to retailers with sustainability requirements, the label provides verification and protects your brand from greenwashing.

### Can my supplier print the FSC label on any paper?
No. The supplier must have a Chain of Custody certificate, and the paper must come from an FSC-certified mill. The label must match the actual material composition of the paper.

### Does brown kraft paper automatically have an FSC label?
No. The brown color of kraft paper comes from the manufacturing process, not from responsible forest management. You must specifically request FSC-certified kraft paper to get the label.
