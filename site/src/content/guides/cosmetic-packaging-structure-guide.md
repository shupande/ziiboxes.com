Custom cosmetic packaging should start with the product, not the artwork. Lipstick, serum bottles, perfume, skincare jars and beauty sets all need different structures, inserts and finish decisions.

For a useful quote, send the product size, product weight, container material, quantity, destination and sales channel. A beauty product sold in retail may need different packaging from a product shipped in a subscription box.

## Common cosmetic box structures

| Product type | Common structure |
| --- | --- |
| Lipstick, mascara, lip gloss | Folding carton or sleeve |
| Serum bottle or skincare jar | Folding carton with insert or rigid box |
| Perfume bottle | Rigid box, drawer box or folding carton with insert |
| Beauty gift set | Rigid box, drawer box or gift box with insert |
| PR kit | Mailer box or rigid presentation box |

## Folding cartons for single cosmetic products

Folding cartons are common for small retail cosmetic products because they are lightweight, printable and efficient for repeat orders. They work well when the product already has a bottle, tube or jar as primary packaging.

See: [custom folding cartons](/products/custom-folding-cartons/) and [custom cosmetic boxes](/industries/custom-cosmetic-boxes/).

## Rigid boxes for premium sets

Rigid boxes are useful when the product needs a gift-ready feel. They are often used for fragrance, skincare sets, influencer kits and limited launches. The insert should be planned before final artwork because it affects the box size and inner layout.

See: [custom rigid boxes](/products/custom-rigid-boxes/).

## Finishes should support the brand

Beauty packaging often uses matte lamination, soft-touch finish, foil stamping, embossing or spot UV. Keep the finish focused on the logo or key visual area if budget matters. Too many effects can make sampling slower and quotes harder to compare.
