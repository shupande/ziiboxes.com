Subscription box packaging needs repeatable packing more than one dramatic sample. The box should be strong enough for shipping, easy to pack and flexible enough for product changes.

Before quoting, send the product mix, box size target, monthly quantity, artwork plan and whether products will be packed in China.

![Custom subscription mailer boxes prepared for repeated ecommerce shipments](/images/industries/custom-subscription-boxes.jpg)

## Subscription box planning checklist

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly product mix | Affects box size, insert layout and packing steps |
| Box size | Repeated shipments make freight volume important |
| Artwork plan | Monthly changes need clear file deadlines |
| Insert or divider | Helps mixed products stay organized |
| Packing method | Decides labor time and quality check steps |
| Carton plan | Needed for warehouse or freight handoff |

## Keep the structure stable

If the box structure changes every month, sampling and packing become slower. A stable box size with artwork changes is often easier than rebuilding the structure for every product mix.

Use dividers or paper inserts only where they solve a real packing problem. Too many inserts can make fulfillment slower.

## When products come from several suppliers

Subscription programs often collect items from multiple suppliers. ZiiBoxes can discuss packaging production, product packing, carton details and delivery to a confirmed China warehouse or freight route when the workflow is suitable.
