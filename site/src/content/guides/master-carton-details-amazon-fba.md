A master pack quantity is usually the number of sellable units inside one shipping case. Pallet quantity is calculated separately from the number of master cartons per layer and the number of layers on the pallet. Write the unit beside every number because “pack quantity” can otherwise mean units, inner packs or cartons.

The basic formulas are:

- Units per master carton = inner packs per carton × units per inner pack
- Master cartons per pallet = cartons per layer × layers per pallet
- Units per pallet = units per master carton × master cartons per pallet

## Packaging levels in plain language

| Level | Also called | What the quantity should describe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each | Base unit or sellable unit | One item sold or handled individually |
| Inner pack | Pack or bundle | Sellable units grouped inside a case |
| Master carton | Case or shipping carton | Total units or inner packs inside one carton |
| Pallet layer | Tie | Master cartons placed on one complete layer |
| Pallet | Load | Total master cartons and total sellable units |

[GS1 describes packaging hierarchies](https://www.gs1.org/standards/gtin-management-guideline-construction-products/current-standard) as base units contained in cases and pallets. Its [pack and case quantity guidance](https://www.gs1.org/1/gtinrules/en/rule/270/packcase-quantity) also shows that changing a defined case or pallet configuration can affect its identification.

## How to calculate master pack and pallet quantity

Suppose one master carton holds 12 sellable units. The pallet fits 10 master cartons on each layer and 5 complete layers.

| Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Master pack quantity | 12 units per master carton |
| Master packs per layer | 10 cartons |
| Layers per pallet | 5 layers |
| Master packs per pallet | 10 × 5 = 50 cartons |
| Total units per pallet | 12 × 50 = 600 units |

This arithmetic is simple, but the usable layer count depends on the real packed carton dimensions, pallet footprint, maximum route height, gross weight and stacking strength. Do not calculate it from the empty retail box.

## Plan cartons before final packing

If the retail box size changes, the master carton plan can change too. This affects carton quantity, freight volume and final landed cost.

Confirm the pallet pattern after a finished master carton is measured and weighed. A layout that fits by dimensions may still be unsuitable if boxes overhang, crush or exceed the route’s weight and height requirements.

For ecommerce and Amazon routes, keep the carton plan simple when possible. Mixed cartons, multiple SKUs and last-minute label changes increase handling risk. Amazon’s current [LTL and FTL delivery guidance](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/1fa2b3b5-802c-43c2-b751-856b4a677724) says boxes should be stacked stably and flush, with labels facing outward. Confirm the live requirements in your Seller Central workflow before shipping because route rules can change.

## Common master pack quantity mistakes

- Entering cartons per pallet where the form asks for units per master carton
- Leaving out whether the number refers to eaches, inner packs or cases
- Calculating pallet layers before the packed carton size is final
- Mixing several SKUs without a clear carton map
- Reusing an old pallet configuration after the product or case quantity changes

## FAQ

### What is master pack quantity?

It usually means the number of sellable units inside one master carton. Confirm the form or customer definition before entering the number.

### What does master pack layers per pallet quantity mean?

It normally asks for the number of complete carton layers stacked on one pallet. It is not the number of cartons on each layer.

### How do I calculate total units on a pallet?

Multiply units per master carton by cartons per layer, then multiply by layers per pallet.

### Can I calculate pallet quantity before production?

You can make an estimate from planned dimensions, but confirm it using a finished, packed and weighed master carton before booking freight.

### Is a master carton the same as a product box?

No. The product box contains one sellable product or set. The master carton is the outer shipping case containing multiple product boxes.
