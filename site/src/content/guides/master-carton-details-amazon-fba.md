Master carton details matter because freight forwarders, warehouses and FBA routes need carton data before goods move. The printed retail box is only one part of the shipment plan.

After products are packed, prepare carton count, carton size, gross weight, units per carton, SKU notes and label requirements.

![Export cartons prepared with carton details for Amazon FBA handoff](/images/shipping-boxes.jpg)

## Carton details to prepare

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Carton size | Used for freight quotes, warehouse receiving and volume planning |
| Gross weight | Needed for freight, handling and warehouse checks |
| Units per carton | Helps avoid SKU and quantity mistakes |
| Carton count | Used for packing lists and handoff notes |
| SKU per carton | Important when several SKUs ship together |
| Carton label | Should match the route confirmed by your team or logistics provider |
| Shipping mark | Useful for supplier, warehouse or freight forwarder receiving |

## Plan cartons before final packing

If the retail box size changes, the master carton plan can change too. This affects carton quantity, freight volume and final landed cost.

For ecommerce and Amazon routes, keep the carton plan simple when possible. Mixed cartons, multiple SKUs and last-minute label changes increase handling risk.
