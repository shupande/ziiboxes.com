Shopify packaging has two jobs: the product packaging presents and contains the item, while the shipping packaging protects the complete order in delivery. Some products can use one printed mailer for both jobs; fragile, retail or gift products often need an inner box plus an outer shipper.

If your products are made in China, confirm the product box, shipping mailer, insert, card, label and carton details before the goods leave the supplier network.

## Shopify packaging checklist

| Item | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Product packaging | Folding carton, sleeve, rigid box, pouch or gift box |
| Shipping packaging | Mailer box, shipping carton or mailer bag |
| Product fit | Product size, weight, fragile points and insert needs |
| Brand details | Outside print, inside print, card, sticker or belly band |
| Customer information | Instructions, warnings, returns and support details |
| Packing steps | Whether products are packed by your factory or a separate packing point |
| China delivery | Supplier address, warehouse contact or freight handoff route |
| Carton plan | Units per carton, carton size, carton weight and label notes |

Shopify’s [product packaging guide](https://www.shopify.com/blog/ecommerce-packaging) also separates the packaging around the product from the outer packaging used for delivery. Making that decision first prevents a presentation box from being treated as a shipping carton when it was not designed for the route.

## Before sampling

Decide whether the packaging is mainly for product protection, retail presentation, direct shipping or a stronger unboxing moment. One box does not need to do every job.

If your product factory will pack the goods, finished boxes can be sent to that factory. If packing should happen separately, send the exact packing steps before quoting.

Use the [paper versus plastic insert comparison](/guides/paper-inserts-vs-plastic-trays/) when products need a fixed layout. For a first launch, compare the [low MOQ packaging options](/guides/low-moq-custom-packaging-small-brands/) before adding several finishes.

## Before production

Check the product fit, insert layout, barcode or label area, and carton size before approving bulk production. A small box-size change can affect shipping volume and packed carton count.

Confirm the final packed sample—not only an empty printed box. Record:

- Approved dieline and artwork version
- Product orientation and packing steps
- Label and barcode position
- Finished pack weight and dimensions
- Units per master carton and carton labels
- Delivery address for the China supplier, warehouse or freight partner

## FAQ

### What packaging is best for Shopify orders?

It depends on the product and route. Printed mailer boxes suit many direct orders; folding cartons and rigid boxes work as inner product packaging when presentation or retail display matters.

### Do Shopify products need both a product box and shipping box?

Not always. A tested mailer may do both jobs for some products. Fragile, premium or retail products often need an inner package and a separate protective shipper.

### What should be included in a Shopify package?

Include only what helps protection, identification and the customer experience: the product package, required labels, instructions, packing slip and any useful insert or card.

### Can my China supplier pack products into Shopify boxes?

Yes, when the supplier or a separate packing point has clear instructions, approved samples, labels and carton requirements.

### What should I send for a Shopify packaging quote?

Send product photos, dimensions, weight, quantity, preferred structure, artwork stage, supplier location, destination and packing route.
