Perfume packaging should be planned with the freight route in mind. Fragrance, liquid and glass details may require extra notes from the logistics provider.

![Export cartons prepared for perfume packaging handoff](/images/shipping-boxes.jpg)

## Details to flag

| Detail | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Liquid or fragrance | Route may ask for product notes |
| Glass bottle | Insert and carton protection |
| Gift set | Mixed SKU and packing list clarity |
| Destination country | Document requirements can differ |
| Freight route | Sea, air, courier or warehouse handoff |
