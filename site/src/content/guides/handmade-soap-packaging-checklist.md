Handmade soap packaging should match the bar size, surface, scent and retail channel. Simple structures often work better than heavy packaging.

![Custom soap packaging boxes for handmade bars](/images/industries/custom-soap-boxes.jpg)

## Quote checklist

| Detail | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Bar size | Length, width, height and weight |
| Box style | Kraft box, window box, sleeve or belly band |
| Label space | Scent, ingredients, barcode or batch label |
| Material | Kraft, white paper or recycled-style paper |
| Packing route | Supplier, packer, warehouse or freight route |

If the soap may transfer oil or scent, raise the material requirement before sampling.
