Necklace and bracelet packaging needs to prevent movement and tangling. The card insert, tab position and box length should be tested before final artwork.

![Custom jewelry box packaging for necklaces and bracelets](/images/industries/custom-jewelry-boxes.jpg)

## Layout checks

| Product | Packaging check |
| --- | --- |
| Necklace | Card holder, chain slot and pendant position |
| Bracelet | Insert tabs and curved product fit |
| Jewelry set | Divider layout and SKU labels |
| Gift packaging | Drawer or rigid box opening feel |
| Ecommerce order | Inner box plus mailer or shipping carton |

Keep the insert layout consistent across SKUs if the same factory packs several necklace or bracelet styles.
