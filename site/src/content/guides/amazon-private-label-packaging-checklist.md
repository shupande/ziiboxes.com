Amazon private label packaging requirements start with a secure sellable unit, a scannable product identifier, correct set or warning labels when needed, and a master-carton plan that matches the shipment route. Exact requirements depend on the product category, destination and the live instructions in your Seller Central account.

This checklist covers the packaging work you should settle with a China supplier before goods move to a warehouse, freight forwarder or FBA route. It does not replace product compliance, customs or account-level Amazon instructions.

## Amazon private label packaging requirements

| Requirement | What to confirm before production |
| --- | --- |
| Secure sellable unit | Product is fully contained and cannot open or separate in normal handling |
| Product fit and protection | Size, weight, fragile points, insert layout and any drop-test need |
| Barcode area | Flat, accessible space for the product barcode or FNSKU label |
| Existing barcodes | Decide which codes must be covered so the intended code is scanned |
| Sets and bundles | Products stay together and carry the required set wording |
| Bags and warnings | Bag size, seal, transparency and any required warning are confirmed |
| Artwork | Dieline, logo, product copy, colors and finish effects are approved |
| Export carton | Units per carton, carton count, carton size and gross weight |
| Handoff route | China supplier, warehouse, freight forwarder or FBA route |

## Before sampling

Decide whether the product factory will pack the goods or send them to a separate China packing point. This changes where empty packaging is delivered, who applies labels and who records the final master-carton data.

If the packaging uses inserts, confirm the product arrangement before artwork. A small insert change can change the box size and carton volume.

## Before printing labels and artwork

Reserve a flat barcode area even if the final file is not ready. Confirm which identifier the fulfillment route expects and whether any manufacturer barcode must be covered.

Do not copy a barcode from a visual reference. Final FNSKU, UPC, shipment and carton labels should come from the system or team responsible for the shipment. Review the [FNSKU and barcode label guide](/guides/fnsku-barcode-label-packaging-china/) before artwork approval.

## Before bulk production

Ask your team or logistics provider to confirm label needs before the packaging is printed. Barcode and FNSKU space should be reserved early, even if the final label file is not ready yet.

For products that may be liquid, powder, battery-related, magnetic, fragile or regulated, raise the issue before packing. The freight route may need extra notes or documents.

Record the approved product package, packing steps, label version, units per master carton, carton dimensions and gross weight. Use the [master pack quantity guide](/guides/master-carton-details-amazon-fba/) to keep units, cartons and pallet quantities clear.

## FAQ

### What are the basic Amazon private label packaging requirements?

The unit should be securely contained, correctly identified, prepared for its product category and packed into labeled master cartons that match the shipment plan.

### Does every private label product need an FNSKU?

The identifier depends on the listing and fulfillment settings. Confirm the current label choice in Seller Central rather than assuming one rule for every SKU.

### Can the product barcode be printed directly on the box?

It may be printed or applied as a label when the final identifier and print quality are confirmed. Leave clear space and test that the intended barcode scans.

### What should be prepared for a product sold as a set?

Keep all pieces securely together and confirm the required set wording so fulfillment staff do not separate them.

### Who confirms the final Amazon requirements?

Your Amazon account, marketplace guidance and logistics route confirm them. The packaging supplier should follow the final files and instructions you provide.
