Perfume packaging should start with the bottle. Bottle size, weight, cap shape and glass protection determine the box and insert.

![Custom perfume packaging boxes for glass fragrance bottles](/images/industries/custom-perfume-boxes.jpg)

## Quote checklist

| Detail | What to confirm |
| --- | --- |
| Bottle size | Height, width, depth and cap shape |
| Bottle weight | Affects insert strength and carton packing |
| Box style | Folding carton, drawer box, rigid box or gift set |
| Insert | Paper insert, divider or stronger support |
| Route notes | Freight, warehouse or paperwork requirements |

Raise any fragrance, liquid or route requirement before packing and freight handoff.
