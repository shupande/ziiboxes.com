Jewelry packaging logo finishes should make the brand mark clear at a small size. Foil, embossing and spot UV can look premium, but only when the logo and material work together.

![Premium gift boxes with foil-style logo finish](/images/products/custom-rigid-boxes.jpg)

## Finish comparison

| Finish | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Foil stamping | Small premium logos and metallic accents |
| Embossing | Raised logo effect on thicker paper |
| Debossing | Subtle pressed logo on rigid boxes |
| Spot UV | Gloss contrast on matte surfaces |
| Printing | Clear, lower-risk logo reproduction |

Ask for a sample when the logo has thin lines or small text. Some effects need simplified artwork.
