Cookie box packaging should protect shape and show the product when appearance matters. Window boxes, trays and dividers are common options.

![Cookie box packaging for bakery and gift food products](/images/cookie-boxes.jpg)

## Cookie box checks

| Detail | Packaging check |
| --- | --- |
| Cookie size | Tray or divider fit |
| Window | Product visibility and box strength |
| Gift set | Insert, card or sleeve layout |
| Direct contact | Inner liner or wrap requirement |
| Shipping route | Outer carton strength |

If cookies are fragile, plan the tray and outer carton together, not as separate decisions.
