Low MOQ packaging means ordering a smaller first production run so you can test the product, artwork and sales channel with less inventory risk. It does not automatically mean cheap packaging: setup, tooling and sampling costs are spread across fewer units, so the unit price is usually higher.

The most practical low MOQ packaging solution is the simplest structure that still protects the product and looks consistent with your brand.

## Low MOQ packaging options compared

| Approach | Best for | Customization | Cost trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stock box plus label or card | Very early testing and samples | Logo label, card or belly band | Lowest setup, limited size control |
| Stock box plus custom sleeve | Seasonal drops and small launches | Full-color sleeve around a standard box | Keeps tooling simple |
| Custom folding carton | Retail products and lightweight goods | Custom size, print and finish | Efficient once size and artwork are stable |
| Custom mailer box | Shopify, DTC and subscription orders | Outside and optional inside print | Larger box size can affect freight |
| Custom rigid box | Premium sets and launch kits | Structure, wrap, insert and finish | Higher setup, assembly and shipping volume |

For small brands, [custom folding cartons](/products/custom-folding-cartons/) and [custom mailer boxes](/products/custom-mailer-boxes/) are often easier starting points than a complex rigid box.

## When a low minimum order makes sense

Low MOQ custom boxes can be practical when:

- You are launching a new product
- Artwork may change after customer feedback
- You are testing retail or wholesale samples
- You have limited storage space
- You need seasonal or event packaging

If you sell through a DTC store, start with the [Shopify packaging checklist](/guides/shopify-packaging-checklist/). It separates the product box, shipping package, insert, label and carton plan so you only customize what the launch needs.

## Why low MOQ packaging costs more per unit

Custom production may require a dieline, cutting tool, print setup, color check, finishing setup and packing labor. Those fixed tasks still exist when the order is small.

The useful comparison is total cash committed versus unit cost. A smaller run can cost more per box but still be the safer business decision if it prevents unused inventory or an artwork reprint.

For a closer look at why minimums change by box style and process, read [what determines custom box MOQ](/guides/custom-box-moq/).

## Ask for a pilot and reorder quote

Instead of asking only “What is your MOQ?”, send your preferred first-run quantity and a realistic reorder quantity. Ask the supplier to quote both with the same size, material and artwork.

This shows where the unit price changes and helps you decide whether to simplify the first run. It also prevents a low headline MOQ from hiding a finish, material or size that is not included.

## Keep the first version simple

For a first order, avoid changing the structure, paper, insert and several finishes at the same time. A correct size, readable artwork and reliable product fit usually matter more than foil, embossing, inside print and specialty paper all at once.

Once the product sells, you can upgrade the next order with a better finish or insert.

## What to send for a useful quote

Send:

- Product photo, dimensions and weight
- Preferred box or bag style
- Pilot quantity and likely reorder quantity
- Destination country and China supplier location, if relevant
- Artwork stage and required launch date
- Any insert, label, finish or product-packing needs

If artwork is not ready, a logo and reference package are enough for an early estimate. Use the [custom packaging quote checklist](/packaging-quote-checklist/) to keep supplier quotes comparable.

When the basic details are ready, [request a packaging quote](/request-a-quote/) with both quantity levels.

## FAQ

### What does low MOQ packaging mean?

It means the supplier will accept a smaller production quantity than its standard bulk run. The exact minimum depends on structure, material, printing, finish and packing method.

### Is low MOQ custom packaging cheaper?

The total order may cost less because you buy fewer units, but the price per unit is usually higher because setup costs are divided across a smaller run.

### Which box style is easiest for a small first order?

Stock packaging with a custom label or sleeve is the simplest. For fully custom paper packaging, folding cartons and mailer boxes are often practical starting points.

### Can I order a small batch and reorder later?

Yes. Keep the approved dieline, artwork and sample details so the supplier can reproduce the same specification. Ask for the likely reorder price before the first order.

### What should I avoid on a low MOQ first run?

Avoid unnecessary structure changes, multiple specialty finishes and an insert that has not been tested with the real product.
